About

Why I built this

RetainHer started with a conversation. A woman told me she'd been trying to grow her hair for years and couldn't figure out why she wasn't seeing progress. She didn't have a log. She didn't have data. She had no way to connect what she was doing to whether it was working.

I talked to 17 more women. Every single one said the same thing. So I built the tool I wish had existed — a tracker that turns your routine into data and your data into clarity.

I'm a solo founder based in Philadelphia. RetainHer is in early access now, and I'm working directly with the first members to make sure it actually changes something.
